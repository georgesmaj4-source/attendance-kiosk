Anti-spoofing (liveness)

Before each punch, the kiosk asks the person to turn their head or smile (chosen at random) and verifies it live — so a printed photo or a face on a phone screen is rejected.

How matching works

Faces are matched on-device using a 128-point face signature — the raw photo is only kept as a small thumbnail for your audit. Each clock event compares the live face to every enrolled employee and picks the closest match within a confidence threshold. Enroll 3+ samples per person (different angles/lighting) for best accuracy.
